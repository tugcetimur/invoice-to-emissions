# UK sample invoices (synthetic)

These nine PDFs are synthetic test invoices created by the repository author. The layouts imitate bills of UK business energy and fuel suppliers.

They are not real invoices. Company names, addresses, VAT numbers, account numbers, bank details and amounts are invented. The companies named on them have nothing to do with this project or with these documents, and their names and trademarks belong to their owners. Please use the files for testing this tool only.

They were used to look at the one-sheet workbook tool and are now a development set for v2. Because the readers and the checks have been examined against them, they cannot serve as evidence of accuracy. The hand-checked findings are in docs/uk-samples-findings.md.
